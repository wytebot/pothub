AI Repair Disclaimer

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Pothub provides automated software diagnostics and code-change suggestions. AI output can be wrong, incomplete, outdated or unsafe.

No guarantee of repair

A diagnosis is not proof of the root cause. A generated patch is not proof that the app builds, passes tests or works in production. Distinguish proposed patches, committed branches, deployment attempts and verified successful deployments.

Human review required

Review affected files, inspect diffs, run tests and review deployment results before merging. Maintain independent backups and use minimum required permissions.

Logs and secrets

Logs and source files may contain credentials or personal information. Remove them before submitting. Automated redaction may miss secrets. Do not process information you are not authorized to share with the configured AI provider.

Third-party services

GitHub, Vercel, Google, Supabase and Flutterwave are independent services. Their policies, availability and pricing may change.

Production readiness

This ZIP is a starter implementation. It requires configuration, security review and testing of OAuth, billing, database policies and deployment behavior before real users or real money are involved.
